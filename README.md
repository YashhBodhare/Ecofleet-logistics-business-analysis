# EcoFleet Logistics — Business Analysis Case Study

A structured business analysis case study for a growing logistics company specializing in eco-friendly transportation. The project explores how digital workflows could help logistics managers assign and monitor delivery tasks, record driver attendance, protect access to operational data, support device compatibility, and give users a clear route to technical help.

> **Project status:** Portfolio case study and working analysis package. Requirements, stakeholder roles, priorities, solution options, and proposed workflows require review and approval by the relevant business and delivery stakeholders before implementation.

## Business context

As EcoFleet Logistics grows, manual or fragmented ways of assigning and tracking work can make it harder for managers to understand task progress, confirm attendance, and respond to user issues. The proposed strategic solution considers secure access to operational data and device compatibility alongside the core task and attendance workflows.

The case study develops a traceable set of business and system analysis artifacts to describe the problem, explore proposed workflows, and prepare the solution for stakeholder validation and UAT.

## Objectives

- Make delivery task assignment and progress tracking clearer for managers and drivers.
- Support a consistent process for recording shift attendance.
- Define secure, role-appropriate access to operational information.
- Identify device compatibility needs and technical support paths.
- Connect requirements to proposed capabilities and UAT scenarios.

## Scope considered

The analysis covers delivery task assignment and tracking, driver attendance, secure operational access, device compatibility, user support, and related business and system requirements.

Vehicle allocation and fleet asset tracking, vehicle maintenance, route optimization, proof of delivery, customer order management, and payroll are outside the current case study scope unless stakeholders approve a change.

## Research and validation notes

- The stakeholder survey folder contains a draft questionnaire and a response tracker. The tracker is intended for recording collected responses; it does not represent 100 completed responses.
- Stakeholder roles and influence assessments are working assumptions and should be validated with actual project stakeholders.
- The As-Is view is bounded by the information available in the case brief. It should not be read as a confirmed description of EcoFleet’s internal operations.
- Proposed To-Be workflows, requirements, priorities, solution options, and UAT cases are drafts for review. They are not approved operating procedures or implementation commitments.
- The UAT tracker is provided for recording test execution, defects, retesting, and sign-off once business users perform testing.

## Suggested review sequence

Start with the project overview and RTM. Review the stakeholder materials and user stories, then follow the wireframes and process flows into the PRD, BRD, FRD, and SRS. Finish with BPMN, gap analysis, solution mapping, and UAT to see how needs connect to proposed capabilities and validation.
